Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Who we are

Mekong Intelligence is a brand and market intelligence firm based in Phnom Penh, Cambodia. This policy explains what information we collect through mekongintel.live and how we use it.

Information you give us

When you send a booking request, we collect:

  • your name and email address;
  • your Telegram username and/or phone number;
  • your company website or Facebook page, if you provide it;
  • your primary goal and the service you are interested in, if you provide them.

We use this only to reply to your request and to discuss working together. We do not sell it or add you to marketing lists without asking.

How your request is handled

Booking requests are delivered to our team through Telegram, and our website is hosted by a third-party provider that keeps short-term server logs. These providers process the information only so that the request reaches us.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, for example which pages are viewed and whether a booking request was sent. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as your approximate location, device and browser. You can block these cookies in your browser settings or with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

How long we keep it

We keep your details only for as long as we need them to respond to you and, if we work together, to manage that relationship.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information you have sent us. Contact us on Telegram at @mekongintelligence.
